02 / Structural Engineering

Exploiting legacy structural margins for maximum vertical density.

Decommissioned modified offshore oil platforms offer unrivaled structural integrity, designed to withstand ocean wave forces and tens of thousands of metric tons of heavy drilling machinery. Modern high-density server racks weigh significantly less than legacy drilling equipment, allowing Nereus to stack a 175,000 square foot building directly onto existing jackets without compromising safety factors.

By erecting a uniform 5-story modular building onto the platform, interconnect routing distance between server racks is drastically shortened while thermal dissipation channels run straight up through the central core.

03 / Thermal Engineering

Titanium Heat Exchangers & Closed-Loop Deep Water Chilling

Mainland data centers consume millions of gallons of freshwater daily in evaporative cooling towers. Nereus completely eliminates freshwater consumption by drawing cold deep sea water directly up into Level 01 heat exchangers. Titanium alloy plates isolate corrosive ocean water from internal closed-loop distilled coolant, yielding unmatched thermal transfer efficiency with zero marine contamination.

04 / Energy Infrastructure

100% Offshore Wind Generation & 9% Emergency Battery Margin

Three heavy-duty offshore turbines positioned surrounding the platform yield roughly 142 million kWh of clean power annually. This supply exceeds the continuous draw of the superstructure, creating a 9% net energy surplus. This surplus is stored in Level 01 solid-state battery banks to guarantee uninterpreted power during low wind conditions.

05 / System Reliability & Disruption Resilience

Engineered for extreme maritime environmental conditions.

Scenario A — Low Wind Event

100% Battery Transition

In the event of zero wind velocity, Level 01 battery banks instantly engage within 2 milliseconds, providing 72 hours of full-load operation without shedding compute workloads.

Scenario B — Category 5 Hurricane

Structural Wave Dissipation

The open steel jacket framework allows massive storm surge waves to pass cleanly through below the deck level without impacting structural stability or data integrity.

Scenario C — Fiber Severance

Dual Subsea + Satellite Mesh

Subsea optical cables are laid in redundant loop paths. If a cable is severed, traffic automatically reroutes to laser satellite arrays on Level 05.
