01 / Structural Engineering

Repurposing existing offshore weight capacity.

Decommissioned oil platforms are structural marvels designed to endure thousands of tons of drilling equipment and rough sea loads. Nereus utilizes these sound steel jacketing systems to house a 5-story stacked modular data building.

By distributing 175,000 square feet across 5 vertical tiers, total physical footprint is minimized while keeping structural center of gravity optimized within the rig's original specs.

02 / Thermal System

Direct Titanium Ocean Heat Exchange

Cold deep-ocean seawater is drawn into low-level titanium heat exchangers before being safely discharged at near-intake depth. Titanium prevents marine corrosion while facilitating continuous passive pre-cooling for internal loops, drastically reducing operational overhead.
